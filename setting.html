ℹ️ About

Daily Routine Tracker v1.0

A simple yet powerful web application to track your daily routines and build consistent habits.

Features:
✓ Create and manage daily routines
✓ Track completion status
✓ View progress analytics
✓ Export/Import data
✓ Category-based organization
✓ Offline-first technology

❓ Help & Support

Getting Started

  • 1️⃣ Click "Add Routine" to create your first routine
  • 2️⃣ Set a title, time, and category for your routine
  • 3️⃣ Mark routines as complete as you finish them
  • 4️⃣ Track progress in the "Track Progress" section

Tips & Tricks

  • 💡 Start with small, achievable routines
  • 💡 Schedule routines at consistent times
  • 💡 Use categories to organize your routines
  • 💡 Review your progress weekly for motivation

🔒 Privacy & Security

Your data is stored locally in your browser. No information is sent to external servers.

Privacy Highlights:
✓ 100% client-side storage
✓ No tracking or analytics
✓ Your data is yours only
✓ Clear local storage anytime
