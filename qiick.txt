📝 DAILY ROUTINE TRACKER - QUICK START GUIDE
=============================================

✅ WHAT YOU HAVE:
- Complete web application for tracking daily routines
- HTML, CSS, JavaScript (works offline)
- Optional PHP/MySQL backend
- Beautiful dark theme UI
- Mobile-responsive design

🚀 TO GET STARTED:

1. OPEN THE APP
   - Simply open "index.html" in your web browser
   - Or run a local server: python -m http.server 8000
   - Then visit http://localhost:8000

2. CREATE YOUR FIRST ROUTINE
   - Click "➕ Add Routine"
   - Fill in the details:
     * Title: e.g., "Morning Meditation"
     * Description: What you'll do
     * Category: Choose a category
     * Date: When to do it
     * Time: What time
     * Frequency: Once, Daily, Weekly, Monthly
   - Click "Save Routine"

3. TRACK YOUR PROGRESS
   - Go to Dashboard to see today's routines
   - Click "Mark Complete" when you finish a routine
   - View your progress on the "Track Progress" page

4. MANAGE YOUR DATA
   - View all routines on "My Routines" page
   - Use filters to find specific routines
   - Export your data as JSON or CSV in Settings
   - Import data from backups
